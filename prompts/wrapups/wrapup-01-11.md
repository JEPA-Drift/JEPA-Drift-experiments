## 1. Context and Discussion Objectives

**Objective.** JEPA-Drift (Article C) tests whether a representation z_t = phi(x_t; C_t) concentrates the Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence of a drift in a daily equity cross-section into a few directions, so that exactly calibrated monitors detect it faster than univariate or pooled monitors. The data-processing inequality (DPI) forbids any KL increase: gains come from concentration only. The proof is semi-synthetic: real panels provide the cross-sections, Monte Carlo backgrounds (D5: Gaussian i.i.d. latent; GARCH(1,1)-t7) and symmetrized real panels (D6: each whole day multiplied by one Rademacher sign) provide the nulls, and the drift families F1-F4 are injected at exact information in return space.

**Organization.** The project is a pipeline of streams (28 planned experiments plus the 8b hygiene and 11b audit follow-ups). Each stream is specified by one canonical prompt in `prompts/streams/`; `prompts/streams/INDEX.md` holds the routing table, the end-of-stream protocol and the replanification of 2026-10-18. The master collection `prompts/Prompts_Claude_Code_v5.md` is derived from the stream files and kept in sync by Mistral Vibe (`tools/check_prompts.py`, a stream 8b deliverable). Two agent families execute the streams: Claude Code (Opus) for the quota-counted streams — 4, 6, 7 done, 12, 13, 14, 22 remaining — and Mistral Vibe for the rest. Each stream runs in its own git worktree on its own branch (`stream-XX`), merged into `main` by fast-forward. Plans are recorded at `prompts/plans/plan-[XX].md` before any code; the researcher's GO reviews live at `prompts/GO-stream-[XX].md`; the wrapups at `prompts/wrapups/wrapup-[XX].md`. The project memory is `CLAUDE.md` (canonical), with `AGENTS.md` as its relative symlink, so both agents load the same configuration.

**Implementation process.** `/plan` with explicit hypotheses and defaults -> the researcher's GO (`prompts/GO-stream-[XX].md`) -> the plan recorded before execution -> TDD execution in the worktree -> wrapup -> reindex (`tools/reindex_graphify_jcodemunch.sh`) -> `make check` -> Conventional Commits without trailers -> push (`git push origin main` from the main tree; `git push origin stream-XX:main` from a worktree). Hard constraints: window 2005-2019 only before milestone J4 (2026-11-08); `.venv/bin/python` (Python 3.12.9, numpy 2.4.4) behind `conda activate Trading`; `SeedSequence`-only randomness; SHA-256 determinism of full runs; CSV outputs via `save_fair_csv`; EUROFIDAI data never committed, logged or pushed.

**Interpretations.**
1. The calibration stack is publishable: exact sign streams on D6, bit-exact Article B identities, Monte Carlo anytime-valid guarantees. This socle is no longer a risk.
2. At real transitions, no breadth information is measurable: the market SR^2 at dated boundaries is selection (the placebo median is ≈ 0), the factor subspace ≈ 0, the idiosyncratic medians are noise-dominated (R9). Consequence: the article's proof is semi-synthetic and the real data serve as support. The R10 trigger of contingency C1 is partially reached; the linear phase of E6 moves to priority A.
3. The bottleneck is detector design, not only information: on F2 at SR = 8 (SYM-D2, constant synthetic characteristics), the mean delays are P7 15 days, P3-C (full residual) 373, P3-CS (b) 446, P3-CS (a) 1,164 — a factor 25 combining the sign efficiency (2/pi), the ln K dictionary tax and the cos^2 alignment between the drift and the channels. The isotropic mixture of Prop. 3, which would avoid the alignment loss, is blocked by the independence gate (a real dependence in factor mode; a multiplicity-only failure in full mode). J2 criterion (iii) is threatened by construction: E1 must measure the alignment to attribute a low slope.
4. N1 ≈ 2 is the most robust channel (N1 1.97 / 2.00; N2 net 1.74-3.04 on F1, 1.45-1.88 on F2; N3 1.21-1.25). P3-CS (b) captures N1 without any learning: the attribution reference for the JEPA, before P3-C.
5. The anytime-valid reading at gamma = 20 is unusable on a long horizon: under H0 on 750 days, the P3-C e-detector alarms on 71 % of trajectories and P2-C on 29 % — a guarantee on ARL0, not on FPR(H). Correction: gamma_H = H/alpha, hence P_inf(alarm before H) <= alpha, comparable with the exact, bootstrap and randomization readings.
6. Central symmetry fails on raw rows (the audit's Holm rejections); the exactness of the sign streams holds on D6, not on the raw market channel.
7. The D3 and D4 universes are survivorship-biased (current S&P 500 constituents; a >= 5,000-observation selection without delisting dates). For Article B, the boundary selection inflates the intra-phase SR: the out-of-budget share of the census (80.3 %) is a lower bound, which strengthens its statement.
